# Evernote Local Viewer — Product Overview

A privacy-first viewer for your Evernote exports. Import your ENEX
export files and browse all your notes locally — nothing leaves your
machine, no cloud account needed.

## Who it's for

Anyone leaving Evernote (or archiving it) who wants to keep their
notes readable, searchable, and private.

## What you can do

- **Import your exports**: bring in one or many ENEX files;
  re-importing the same export won't create duplicates.
- **Browse your notes**: the notes list is sorted by last-modified
  date so recent notes are on top.
- **Read everything**: full note content renders as it did in
  Evernote, including formatting.
- **Download attachments**: files attached to notes can be
  downloaded per note.
- **Search and filter**: search currently works on note titles and
  metadata; you can also filter by which export file a note came
  from, or show only notes with attachments.
